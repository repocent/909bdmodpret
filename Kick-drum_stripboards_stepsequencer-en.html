      Double ring kick (own design)

      Own design on 45 strips: two detuned sine rings (body + beater), a click pad, a mixer and a diode clipper drive. Three TL op-amps in total.

      Own design, not built. The layout is internally checked (netlist below, derived from the strips). There is no true pitch envelope: the rapidly decaying higher beater ring sits on top of the body ring.

        TR-909 with common mods

        The 909 kick extended with the best-known bass drum mods: PITCH, TUNE DEPTH, DRIVE, extended TUNE/DECAY and DECAY HOLD.

        The sound is a model, not a circuit simulation; the mod controls are my interpretation of what the mods do according to the sources.
